Send friends the invite link. They create an account, open the link and press Join. The host starts the game once everyone's in (minimum 2 players).

    Choose your class

    Hidden from other players until the game ends. If you don't pick one, you get a random class.

    Game settings

    Limits: board 3–50, turn timer 1 second to 7 days, AP drops every 1 second to 30 days. For drops less than a day apart, the timing lines up with the time of day (e.g. every 6 hours from 5pm = 5am, 11am, 5pm, 11pm).

    Edges wrap around. Shaded squares are within your range. Click a tank to target it.
